# KreativeTab design system

## Direction

Modern, spacious and editorial. The interface should feel confident and inventive without sacrificing clarity. Use flat colors only—never gradients, glass effects or ornamental shadows.

## Colors

- Black `#000000`: primary headings, links, highlights and strong brand surfaces
- Flare Green `#8BE67D`: primary calls to action and sparse accents
- Ink `#0E1330`: primary body text and dark surfaces
- Paper `#F8F7F3`: main background
- Charcoal `#2F393D`: secondary body text
- White `#FFFFFF`: contrast surfaces and text

Only use flat colors and 10%, 25%, 50% and 75% tints.

## Typography

- Headings: Plus Jakarta Sans, weights 600 and 700
- Body: Inter, weights 400, 500 and 600
- Scale: 9, 12, 16, 21, 28, 38, 51 and 67px
- Use responsive `clamp()` values for display typography

## Interface rules

- Maintain WCAG AA contrast and visible keyboard focus.
- Interactive targets must be at least 44×44px.
- Use rounded corners selectively, with 12–16px as the normal range.
- Motion should be subtle, last 150–450ms and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Lead with a clear promise, credible work and one primary conversion action.
